About IndigoMx

Critical synthesis and communication of science, for better health

A boutique scientific, healthcare communication and consulting agency, established in June 2019 in the Philippines.

Who we are

Small by design, rigorous by nature

IndigoMx was founded by Dr. Ivan Olegario to meet the need for more thoughtful, evidence-based and user-centric healthcare communications and scientific services.

We work with healthcare organisations across Southeast Asia, the Western Pacific and the Middle East, either directly or in partnership with other scientific partners. As a boutique team, every project gets senior attention from start to finish.

About the founder

Ivan Olegario, MD, MDC, CMPP

Dr. Ivan Olegario is an ISMPP-certified medical writer with almost 20 years of experience in healthcare communications for private industry, the public health sector and non-governmental organisations.

As a medical writer, he has worked with healthcare communications agencies including MIMS, McCann Health, Ogilvy/GCI Health, Commune Healthcare, ContentEdNet and Huntsworth Health. In industry, he has experience in medical affairs, pharmaceutical marketing and sales, business development, strategy, planning and forecasting. He has also led writing groups for ministries of health, medical societies, the World Health Organization and the Joint United Nations Programme on HIV/AIDS (UNAIDS).

He earned his medical degree with honours from the University of the Philippines College of Medicine via the accelerated track, followed by a Master of Development Communication from the same university. He also completed the Management Development Programme of the Asian Institute of Management.

  • Master of Development Communication
  • Certified Medical Publication Professional
  • AIM Management Development Programme
