Dr. Ivan Olegario Founder and Lead Consultant
Led by a physician-writer

A mind for science.
A feel for communication.

Dr. Ivan Olegario is an ISMPP-certified medical writer with almost 20 years of experience in healthcare communications across private industry, the public health sector and non-governmental organisations.

He has worked with agencies including MIMS, McCann Health, Ogilvy/GCI Health and Huntsworth Health, and has led writing groups for ministries of health, medical societies, the World Health Organization and UNAIDS.

  • MD, University of the Philippines
